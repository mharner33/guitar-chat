# Major Second

The **major second** (M2) spans **2 semitones** (1 whole step). Measured up from C it is **C–D**.

It is a mild dissonance. Inverting it within the octave gives the [Minor Seventh](/intervals/minor-seventh.md).

*Also called:* whole step, tone.
