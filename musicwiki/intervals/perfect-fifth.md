# Perfect Fifth

The **perfect fifth** (P5) spans **7 semitones** (3 whole + 1 half step). Measured up from C it is **C–G**.

It is a perfect consonance. Inverting it within the octave gives the [Perfect Fourth](/intervals/perfect-fourth.md).
