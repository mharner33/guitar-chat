# Bb Minor 9th Chord

The Bb minor 9th chord is a minor seventh extended with the ninth — smooth and modern. Its tones are **Bb – Db – F – Ab – C**: Bb (root), Db (minor third), F (perfect fifth), Ab (minor seventh), C (ninth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bb | root |
| b3 | Db | minor third |
| 5 | F | perfect fifth |
| b7 | Ab | minor seventh |
| 9 | C | ninth |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `6-4-6-5-6-4` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 4. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
