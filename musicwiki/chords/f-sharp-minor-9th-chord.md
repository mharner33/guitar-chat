# F# Minor 9th Chord

The F# minor 9th chord is a minor seventh extended with the ninth — smooth and modern. Its tones are **F# – A – C# – E – G#**: F# (root), A (minor third), C# (perfect fifth), E (minor seventh), G# (ninth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | F# | root |
| b3 | A | minor third |
| 5 | C# | perfect fifth |
| b7 | E | minor seventh |
| 9 | G# | ninth |
