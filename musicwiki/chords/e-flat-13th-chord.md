# Eb 13th Chord

The Eb 13th chord is a dominant chord extended through the thirteenth — the fullest dominant color. Its tones are **Eb – G – Bb – Db – F – C**: Eb (root), G (major third), Bb (perfect fifth), Db (minor seventh), F (ninth), C (thirteenth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eb | root |
| 3 | G | major third |
| 5 | Bb | perfect fifth |
| b7 | Db | minor seventh |
| 9 | F | ninth |
| 13 | C | thirteenth |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `11-10-10-10-11-9` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 9. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
