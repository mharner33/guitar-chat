# Db 11th Chord

The Db 11th chord is a dominant chord extended through the eleventh. Its tones are **Db – F – Ab – Cb – Eb – Gb**: Db (root), F (major third), Ab (perfect fifth), Cb (minor seventh), Eb (ninth), Gb (eleventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Db | root |
| 3 | F | major third |
| 5 | Ab | perfect fifth |
| b7 | Cb | minor seventh |
| 9 | Eb | ninth |
| 11 | Gb | eleventh |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `x-4-3-4-4-2` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 2. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
