# A# Minor 7th Flat 5 Chord

The A# minor 7th flat 5 chord is the half-diminished chord: a diminished triad plus a minor seventh, the ii chord of a minor key. Its tones are **A# – C# – E – G#**: A# (root), C# (minor third), E (diminished fifth), G# (minor seventh).

|---|---|---|
| 1 | A# | root |
| b3 | C# | minor third |
| b5 | E | diminished fifth |
| b7 | G# | minor seventh |
