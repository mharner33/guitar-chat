# Bb Major 9th Chord

The Bb major 9th chord is a major seventh extended with the ninth — expansive and warm. Its tones are **Bb – D – F – A – C**: Bb (root), D (major third), F (perfect fifth), A (major seventh), C (ninth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bb | root |
| 3 | D | major third |
| 5 | F | perfect fifth |
| 7 | A | major seventh |
| 9 | C | ninth |
