# B 13th Chord

The B 13th chord is a dominant chord extended through the thirteenth — the fullest dominant color. Its tones are **B – D# – F# – A – C# – G#**: B (root), D# (major third), F# (perfect fifth), A (minor seventh), C# (ninth), G# (thirteenth).
