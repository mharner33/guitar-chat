# Ab Minor Major 7th Chord

The Ab minor major 7th chord is a minor triad plus a major seventh — a haunting, unstable color. Its tones are **Ab – Cb – Eb – G**: Ab (root), Cb (minor third), Eb (perfect fifth), G (major seventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | root |
| b3 | Cb | minor third |
| 5 | Eb | perfect fifth |
| 7 | G | major seventh |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `4-2-1-0-0-3` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 1. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
