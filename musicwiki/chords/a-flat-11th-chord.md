# Ab 11th Chord

The Ab 11th chord is a dominant chord extended through the eleventh. Its tones are **Ab – C – Eb – Gb – Bb – Db**: Ab (root), C (major third), Eb (perfect fifth), Gb (minor seventh), Bb (ninth), Db (eleventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | root |
| 3 | C | major third |
| 5 | Eb | perfect fifth |
| b7 | Gb | minor seventh |
| 9 | Bb | ninth |
| 11 | Db | eleventh |
