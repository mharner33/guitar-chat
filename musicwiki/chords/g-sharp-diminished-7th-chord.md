# G# Diminished 7th Chord

The G# diminished 7th chord is four stacked minor thirds — symmetrical and maximally tense, able to resolve many directions. Its tones are **G# – B – D – F**: G# (root), B (minor third), D (diminished fifth), F (diminished seventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | G# | root |
| b3 | B | minor third |
| b5 | D | diminished fifth |
| bb7 | F | diminished seventh |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `4-2-3-4-3-4` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 2. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
