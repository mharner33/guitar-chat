# Gb Major 9th Chord

The Gb major 9th chord is a major seventh extended with the ninth — expansive and warm. Its tones are **Gb – Bb – Db – F – Ab**: Gb (root), Bb (major third), Db (perfect fifth), F (major seventh), Ab (ninth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gb | root |
| 3 | Bb | major third |
| 5 | Db | perfect fifth |
| 7 | F | major seventh |
| 9 | Ab | ninth |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `2-1-3-1-2-1` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 1. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
