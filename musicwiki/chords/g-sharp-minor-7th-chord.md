# G# Minor 7th Chord

The G# minor 7th chord is a minor triad plus a minor seventh — smooth and mellow, the workhorse of jazz and soul. Its tones are **G# – B – D# – F#**: G# (root), B (minor third), D# (perfect fifth), F# (minor seventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | G# | root |
| b3 | B | minor third |
| 5 | D# | perfect fifth |
| b7 | F# | minor seventh |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `4-2-4-4-4-2` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 2. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
