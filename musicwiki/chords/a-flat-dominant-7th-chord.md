# Ab Dominant 7th Chord

The Ab dominant 7th chord is a major triad plus a minor seventh — the restless engine of tonal harmony, pulling toward the tonic a fourth above. Its tones are **Ab – C – Eb – Gb**: Ab (root), C (major third), Eb (perfect fifth), Gb (minor seventh).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | root |
| 3 | C | major third |
| 5 | Eb | perfect fifth |
| b7 | Gb | minor seventh |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `4-3-1-1-1-2` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 1. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
