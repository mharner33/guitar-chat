# Bb 13th Chord

The Bb 13th chord is a dominant chord extended through the thirteenth — the fullest dominant color. Its tones are **Bb – D – F – Ab – C – G**: Bb (root), D (major third), F (perfect fifth), Ab (minor seventh), C (ninth), G (thirteenth).
