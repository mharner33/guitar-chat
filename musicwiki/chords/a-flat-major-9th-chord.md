# Ab Major 9th Chord

The Ab major 9th chord is a major seventh extended with the ninth — expansive and warm. Its tones are **Ab – C – Eb – G – Bb**: Ab (root), C (major third), Eb (perfect fifth), G (major seventh), Bb (ninth).

| Interval | Note | Name |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | root |
| 3 | C | major third |
| 5 | Eb | perfect fifth |
| 7 | G | major seventh |
| 9 | Bb | ninth |

**Playing it:** the primary shape is `4-1-1-0-1-3` (strings low→high, `x` = muted), around fret 1. 3 computed fingerings are in the render block, with alphaTex playback.
