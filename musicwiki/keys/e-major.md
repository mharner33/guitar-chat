# E Major

The key of **E Major** is built on the [E Major Scale](/scales/e-major-scale.md). Stacking thirds on each scale degree gives its diatonic chords:

| Roman | Triad | Seventh | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | [E major](/triads/e-major-triad.md) | [E Major 7th Chord](/chords/e-major-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| ii | [F# minor](/triads/f-sharp-minor-triad.md) | [F# Minor 7th Chord](/chords/f-sharp-minor-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| iii | [G# minor](/triads/g-sharp-minor-triad.md) | [G# Minor 7th Chord](/chords/g-sharp-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| IV | [A major](/triads/a-major-triad.md) | [A Major 7th Chord](/chords/a-major-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| V | [B major](/triads/b-major-triad.md) | [B Dominant 7th Chord](/chords/b-dominant-7th-chord.md) | dominant |
| vi | [C# minor](/triads/c-sharp-minor-triad.md) | [C# Minor 7th Chord](/chords/c-sharp-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| vii° | [D# diminished](/triads/d-sharp-diminished-triad.md) | [D# Minor 7th Flat 5 Chord](/chords/d-sharp-minor-7th-flat-5-chord.md) | dominant |

Roman numerals are upper-case for major, lower-case for minor, `°` for diminished. The **dominant** (V) pulls to the **tonic** (I); **predominant** chords (ii, IV) set up the dominant.
