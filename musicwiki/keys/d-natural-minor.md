# D Natural Minor

The key of **D Natural Minor** is built on the [D Natural Minor Scale](/scales/d-natural-minor-scale.md). Stacking thirds on each scale degree gives its diatonic chords:

| Roman | Triad | Seventh | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| i | [D minor](/triads/d-minor-triad.md) | [D Minor 7th Chord](/chords/d-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| ii° | [E diminished](/triads/e-diminished-triad.md) | [E Minor 7th Flat 5 Chord](/chords/e-minor-7th-flat-5-chord.md) | predominant |
| III | [F major](/triads/f-major-triad.md) | [F Major 7th Chord](/chords/f-major-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| iv | [G minor](/triads/g-minor-triad.md) | [G Minor 7th Chord](/chords/g-minor-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| v | [A minor](/triads/a-minor-triad.md) | [A Minor 7th Chord](/chords/a-minor-7th-chord.md) | dominant |
| VI | [Bb major](/triads/b-flat-major-triad.md) | [Bb Major 7th Chord](/chords/b-flat-major-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| VII | [C major](/triads/c-major-triad.md) | [C Dominant 7th Chord](/chords/c-dominant-7th-chord.md) | subtonic |

Roman numerals are upper-case for major, lower-case for minor, `°` for diminished. The **dominant** (V) pulls to the **tonic** (I); **predominant** chords (ii, IV) set up the dominant.
