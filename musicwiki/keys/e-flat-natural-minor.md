# Eb Natural Minor

The key of **Eb Natural Minor** is built on the [Eb Natural Minor Scale](/scales/e-flat-natural-minor-scale.md). Stacking thirds on each scale degree gives its diatonic chords:

| Roman | Triad | Seventh | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| i | [Eb minor](/triads/e-flat-minor-triad.md) | [Eb Minor 7th Chord](/chords/e-flat-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| ii° | [F diminished](/triads/f-diminished-triad.md) | [F Minor 7th Flat 5 Chord](/chords/f-minor-7th-flat-5-chord.md) | predominant |
| III | [Gb major](/triads/g-flat-major-triad.md) | [Gb Major 7th Chord](/chords/g-flat-major-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| iv | [Ab minor](/triads/g-sharp-minor-triad.md) | [G# Minor 7th Chord](/chords/g-sharp-minor-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| v | [Bb minor](/triads/b-flat-minor-triad.md) | [Bb Minor 7th Chord](/chords/b-flat-minor-7th-chord.md) | dominant |
| VI | [Cb major](/triads/b-major-triad.md) | [B Major 7th Chord](/chords/b-major-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| VII | [Db major](/triads/d-flat-major-triad.md) | [Db Dominant 7th Chord](/chords/d-flat-dominant-7th-chord.md) | subtonic |

Roman numerals are upper-case for major, lower-case for minor, `°` for diminished. The **dominant** (V) pulls to the **tonic** (I); **predominant** chords (ii, IV) set up the dominant.
