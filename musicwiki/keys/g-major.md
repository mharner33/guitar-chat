# G Major

The key of **G Major** is built on the [G Major Scale](/scales/g-major-scale.md). Stacking thirds on each scale degree gives its diatonic chords:

| Roman | Triad | Seventh | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | [G major](/triads/g-major-triad.md) | [G Major 7th Chord](/chords/g-major-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| ii | [A minor](/triads/a-minor-triad.md) | [A Minor 7th Chord](/chords/a-minor-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| iii | [B minor](/triads/b-minor-triad.md) | [B Minor 7th Chord](/chords/b-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| IV | [C major](/triads/c-major-triad.md) | [C Major 7th Chord](/chords/c-major-7th-chord.md) | predominant |
| V | [D major](/triads/d-major-triad.md) | [D Dominant 7th Chord](/chords/d-dominant-7th-chord.md) | dominant |
| vi | [E minor](/triads/e-minor-triad.md) | [E Minor 7th Chord](/chords/e-minor-7th-chord.md) | tonic |
| vii° | [F# diminished](/triads/f-sharp-diminished-triad.md) | [F# Minor 7th Flat 5 Chord](/chords/f-sharp-minor-7th-flat-5-chord.md) | dominant |

Roman numerals are upper-case for major, lower-case for minor, `°` for diminished. The **dominant** (V) pulls to the **tonic** (I); **predominant** chords (ii, IV) set up the dominant.
