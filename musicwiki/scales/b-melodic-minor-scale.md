# B Melodic Minor Scale

The B melodic minor scale is a natural minor with raised sixth and seventh, smoothing the melodic ascent to the tonic. It is spelled **B – C# – D – E – F# – G# – A#**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–W–W–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | C# | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | D | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | E | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | F# | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | G# | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | A# | 7 | leading-tone |
