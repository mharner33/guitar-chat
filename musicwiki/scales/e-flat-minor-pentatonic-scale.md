# Eb Minor Pentatonic Scale

The Eb minor pentatonic scale is the five-note minor scale (1 b3 4 5 b7), the backbone of blues and rock lead guitar. It is spelled **Eb – Gb – Ab – Bb – Db**.

**Step pattern:** W½–W–W–W½–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eb | 1 |
| 2 | Gb | b3 |
| 3 | Ab | 4 |
| 4 | Bb | 5 |
| 5 | Db | b7 |
