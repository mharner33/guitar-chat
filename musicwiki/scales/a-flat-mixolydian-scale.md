# Ab Mixolydian Scale

The Ab mixolydian scale is a major mode with a lowered seventh — the sound of dominant chords and blues-rock. It is spelled **Ab – Bb – C – Db – Eb – F – Gb**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–H–W–W–H–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | Bb | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | C | 3 | mediant |
| 4 | Db | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | Eb | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | F | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | Gb | b7 | subtonic |
