# C Major Scale

The C major scale is the bright, resolved reference scale of Western music — every other scale is heard against it. It is spelled **C – D – E – F – G – A – B**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–H–W–W–W–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | D | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | E | 3 | mediant |
| 4 | F | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | G | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | A | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | B | 7 | leading-tone |
