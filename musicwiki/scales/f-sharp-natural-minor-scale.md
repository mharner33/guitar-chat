# F# Natural Minor Scale

The F# natural minor scale is the dark, melancholic minor scale, identical to the Aeolian mode. It is spelled **F# – G# – A – B – C# – D – E**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F# | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | G# | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | A | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | B | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | C# | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | D | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | E | b7 | subtonic |
