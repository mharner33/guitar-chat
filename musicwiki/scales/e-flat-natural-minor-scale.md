# Eb Natural Minor Scale

The Eb natural minor scale is the dark, melancholic minor scale, identical to the Aeolian mode. It is spelled **Eb – F – Gb – Ab – Bb – Cb – Db**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eb | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | F | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Gb | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | Ab | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | Bb | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | Cb | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | Db | b7 | subtonic |
