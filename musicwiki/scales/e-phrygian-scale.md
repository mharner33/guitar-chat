# E Phrygian Scale

The E phrygian scale is a dark minor mode with a lowered second, giving a Spanish/flamenco color. It is spelled **E – F – G – A – B – C – D**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | F | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | G | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | A | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | B | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | C | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | D | b7 | subtonic |
