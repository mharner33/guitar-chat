# C# Harmonic Minor Scale

The C# harmonic minor scale is a natural minor with a raised seventh, giving a strong leading tone and an exotic augmented second between the sixth and seventh degrees. It is spelled **C# – D# – E – F# – G# – A – B#**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W½–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C# | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | D# | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | E | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | F# | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | G# | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | A | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | B# | 7 | leading-tone |
