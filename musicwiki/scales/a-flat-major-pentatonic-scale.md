# Ab Major Pentatonic Scale

The Ab major pentatonic scale is the five-note major scale (degrees 1 2 3 5 6), dropping the half-steps for an open, consonant sound ubiquitous in folk, country, and rock. It is spelled **Ab – Bb – C – Eb – F**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–W½–W–W½  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ab | 1 |
| 2 | Bb | 2 |
| 3 | C | 3 |
| 4 | Eb | 5 |
| 5 | F | 6 |
