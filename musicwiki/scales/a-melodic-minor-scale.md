# A Melodic Minor Scale

The A melodic minor scale is a natural minor with raised sixth and seventh, smoothing the melodic ascent to the tonic. It is spelled **A – B – C – D – E – F# – G#**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–W–W–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | B | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | C | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | D | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | E | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | F# | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | G# | 7 | leading-tone |
