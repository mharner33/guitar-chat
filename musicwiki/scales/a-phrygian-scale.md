# A Phrygian Scale

The A phrygian scale is a dark minor mode with a lowered second, giving a Spanish/flamenco color. It is spelled **A – Bb – C – D – E – F – G**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | Bb | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | C | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | D | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | E | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | F | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | G | b7 | subtonic |
