# Bb Phrygian Scale

The Bb phrygian scale is a dark minor mode with a lowered second, giving a Spanish/flamenco color. It is spelled **Bb – Cb – Db – Eb – F – Gb – Ab**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bb | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | Cb | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Db | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | Eb | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | F | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | Gb | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | Ab | b7 | subtonic |
