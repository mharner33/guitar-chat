# Eb Major Scale

The Eb major scale is the bright, resolved reference scale of Western music — every other scale is heard against it. It is spelled **Eb – F – G – Ab – Bb – C – D**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–H–W–W–W–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eb | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | F | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | G | 3 | mediant |
| 4 | Ab | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | Bb | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | C | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | D | 7 | leading-tone |
