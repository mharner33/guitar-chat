# D# Blues Scale

The D# blues scale is the minor pentatonic with an added b5 'blue note' (1 b3 4 b5 5 b7) — the sound of blues and rock soloing. It is spelled **D# – F# – G# – A – A# – C#**.

**Step pattern:** W½–W–H–H–W½–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | D# | 1 |
| 2 | F# | b3 |
| 3 | G# | 4 |
| 4 | A | b5 |
| 5 | A# | 5 |
| 6 | C# | b7 |
