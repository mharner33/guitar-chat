# D Lydian Scale

The D lydian scale is a major mode with a raised fourth — bright and dreamlike. It is spelled **D – E – F# – G# – A – B – C#**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–W–H–W–W–H  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | E | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | F# | 3 | mediant |
| 4 | G# | b5 | subdominant |
| 5 | A | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | B | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | C# | 7 | leading-tone |
