# B Locrian Scale

The B locrian scale is the darkest mode, built on a diminished tonic — rarely used as a tonal center. It is spelled **B – C – D – E – F – G – A**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–H–W–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | C | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | D | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | E | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | F | b5 | dominant |
| 6 | G | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | A | b7 | subtonic |
