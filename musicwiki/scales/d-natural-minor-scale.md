# D Natural Minor Scale

The D natural minor scale is the dark, melancholic minor scale, identical to the Aeolian mode. It is spelled **D – E – F – G – A – Bb – C**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | E | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | F | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | G | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | A | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | Bb | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | C | b7 | subtonic |
