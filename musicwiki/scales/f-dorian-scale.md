# F Dorian Scale

The F dorian scale is a minor mode with a raised sixth — a staple of jazz, folk, and modal rock. It is spelled **F – G – Ab – Bb – C – D – Eb**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–W–H–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | G | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Ab | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | Bb | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | C | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | D | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | Eb | b7 | subtonic |
