# F# Phrygian Scale

The F# phrygian scale is a dark minor mode with a lowered second, giving a Spanish/flamenco color. It is spelled **F# – G – A – B – C# – D – E**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F# | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | G | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | A | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | B | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | C# | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | D | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | E | b7 | subtonic |
