# C Locrian Scale

The C locrian scale is the darkest mode, built on a diminished tonic — rarely used as a tonal center. It is spelled **C – Db – Eb – F – Gb – Ab – Bb**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–H–W–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | Db | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Eb | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | F | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | Gb | b5 | dominant |
| 6 | Ab | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | Bb | b7 | subtonic |
