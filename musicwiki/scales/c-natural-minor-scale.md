# C Natural Minor Scale

The C natural minor scale is the dark, melancholic minor scale, identical to the Aeolian mode. It is spelled **C – D – Eb – F – G – Ab – Bb**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | D | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Eb | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | F | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | G | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | Ab | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | Bb | b7 | subtonic |
