# D Mixolydian Scale

The D mixolydian scale is a major mode with a lowered seventh — the sound of dominant chords and blues-rock. It is spelled **D – E – F# – G – A – B – C**.

**Step pattern:** W–W–H–W–W–H–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | D | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | E | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | F# | 3 | mediant |
| 4 | G | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | A | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | B | 6 | submediant |
| 7 | C | b7 | subtonic |
