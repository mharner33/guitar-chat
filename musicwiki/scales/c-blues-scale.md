# C Blues Scale

The C blues scale is the minor pentatonic with an added b5 'blue note' (1 b3 4 b5 5 b7) — the sound of blues and rock soloing. It is spelled **C – Eb – F – Gb – G – Bb**.

**Step pattern:** W½–W–H–H–W½–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | 1 |
| 2 | Eb | b3 |
| 3 | F | 4 |
| 4 | Gb | b5 |
| 5 | G | 5 |
| 6 | Bb | b7 |
