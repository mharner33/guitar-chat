# B Natural Minor Scale

The B natural minor scale is the dark, melancholic minor scale, identical to the Aeolian mode. It is spelled **B – C# – D – E – F# – G – A**.

**Step pattern:** W–H–W–W–H–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | B | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | C# | 2 | supertonic |
| 3 | D | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | E | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | F# | 5 | dominant |
| 6 | G | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | A | b7 | subtonic |
