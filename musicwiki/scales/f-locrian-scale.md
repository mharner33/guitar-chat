# F Locrian Scale

The F locrian scale is the darkest mode, built on a diminished tonic — rarely used as a tonal center. It is spelled **F – Gb – Ab – Bb – Cb – Db – Eb**.

**Step pattern:** H–W–W–H–W–W–W  *(W = whole, H = half, W½ = one-and-a-half steps)*

| Degree | Note | Interval | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | F | 1 | tonic |
| 2 | Gb | b2 | supertonic |
| 3 | Ab | b3 | mediant |
| 4 | Bb | 4 | subdominant |
| 5 | Cb | b5 | dominant |
| 6 | Db | b6 | submediant |
| 7 | Eb | b7 | subtonic |
